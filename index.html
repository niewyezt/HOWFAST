Privacy Policy – HOWFAST

App: HOWFAST

Effective date: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the HOWFAST mobile application (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to measure speed, distance, acceleration, route, altitude and similar movement or performance-related data using your Android device.

In plain English:
HOWFAST uses location data and device sensors to calculate measurement results inside the App. The developer does not run a custom backend for ordinary app use and does not sell your data. Most measurement results, settings and history are intended to stay on your device. If you use premium features, purchases, subscriptions or free trials, purchase-related data may be processed by Google Play and RevenueCat.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store certain information locally on your device, such as:

  • app settings and preferences,
  • selected units, modes, warning thresholds and measurement options,
  • speed, distance, time, acceleration, altitude, route or track-related measurement results,
  • measurement history, records, snapshots or app-generated summaries,
  • locally cached app data needed for normal functionality.

This locally stored information is generally not transmitted to the developer’s own servers. You can remove locally stored data by using available in-app delete/clean options, clearing the App’s data in Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

3. Location Data

The App may request access to your device’s location in order to calculate speed, distance, route, altitude, movement, travel path and related measurement results. Depending on the feature, the App may require location access while the App is in use.

Location access is used to provide the App’s core functionality. If you deny location permission, speed, route, distance or performance measurements may be unavailable or limited.

The developer does not use a custom backend to store your location data from ordinary use of the App. Location data used for measurements is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share or export it using Android or other apps installed on your device.

4. Device Sensors

The App may use device sensors such as accelerometer, gyroscope, orientation sensors or similar Android sensor data to support measurement features, improve detection of movement, acceleration or braking, and provide performance-related results.

Sensor data is used for App functionality and is generally processed locally on your device.

5. Measurement History, Export and Sharing

The App may allow you to save, view, delete, export or share measurement results, history records, route snapshots, screenshots or similar app-generated content.

When you choose to share or export data, the information is passed to the app or service selected by you through the Android sharing mechanism or another export method. The developer is not responsible for how third-party apps selected by you process shared content.

Do not operate the App in a way that distracts you while driving, riding, flying, moving or operating machinery. Always follow traffic laws, safety rules and applicable regulations.
