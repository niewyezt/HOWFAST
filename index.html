Privacy Policy for HOWFAST

Last updated: May 2026

HOWFAST is a speed and performance measurement application for Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user data.

1. Data collected by the app

HOWFAST may use device sensors and location data to calculate speed, distance, acceleration, route, altitude or similar measurement results. This data is used inside the app to provide its core functionality.

2. Location data

The app may request access to location data in order to measure speed, distance, route and related driving or movement parameters. Location data is used for app functionality and is not sold by the developer.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

HOWFAST may offer premium features through subscriptions or trial access. Payments and subscription management are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat. The developer does not directly process payment card details.

4. Third-party services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play and RevenueCat for billing, subscription status verification and app distribution.

5. Data sharing

The developer does not sell personal data. Some technical or purchase-related data may be processed by Google Play or RevenueCat as required to provide billing and subscription functionality.

6. Data security

Reasonable measures are used to protect app-related data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

HOWFAST is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through the Google Play listing or the official support channel provided there.
